HackerTube privacy policy and terms — version 2026-11-08.1

HackerTube is an independent, locally installed Hermes plugin for discovering videos, podcasts, books and audiobooks. It uses YouTube API Services when you enable YouTube. By using HackerTube you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms . Google’s Privacy Policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy . HackerTube is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.

Explicit Search sends your question, inferred topic and bounded retrieved titles, contributors, IDs and source metadata to your selected Hermes model provider and configured web-search service. Enabled catalog services (Hacker News/Algolia, Apple Search, Open Library, LibriVox, YouTube and optional Podcast Index) receive the search topic and ordinary request information such as your IP address. Open Library alone receives your privately configured contact in its request header. Those providers apply their own privacy policies. Already accepted remote requests can continue processing or billing after local cancellation.

HackerTube stores questions, job history and usage, normalized source metadata, interests, saved/dismissed/completed flags, source choices, source-open history and this policy agreement in this Hermes profile’s local plugin database. It requests no unrelated chats, Hermes memory, external watch history, Google login, Google OAuth token or YouTube account data. Hermes manages model authentication and declared source keys. No HackerTube telemetry, advertising or embedded media player is included.

Local browsing and library actions make no model or search request. A permitted Open Library cover hotlink sends your IP and browser request information to its image service. Opening an original source leaves HackerTube; that destination may use its own cookies, advertising and tracking. HackerTube’s page uses in-memory UI state, not its own tracking cookies. Hermes supplies its own authentication and storage controls.

Source metadata expires within 30 days. While Hermes is running, local housekeeping checks known profiles every hour; every profile opening and metadata access also purges expired records before display. A stopped computer or stopped Hermes cannot execute deletion; cleanup runs before the next access after restart. Do not retain exported database backups beyond that period. No background paid refresh runs. A new explicit YouTube Search replaces the current metadata for returned IDs and removes older stored copies for IDs checked as deleted/private; saved flags remain independent. Retrieval times are shown. Relevance explanations and library state are HackerTube features, not supplied or endorsed by YouTube.

Use “Delete HackerTube data and revoke agreement” in Privacy to erase this profile’s plugin-owned metadata, questions, jobs, usage, interests, library history, source choices and agreement. First cancel any active Search. Deletion is immediate in the local database and does not delete anything held by YouTube, other providers, your backups, or Hermes’s separately managed credentials/logs. Remove source keys in Hermes settings if desired. No Google OAuth authorization is requested; if you use other authorized Google applications, their permissions can be managed at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions .

The full policy is bundled and always available through Privacy before agreement and after installation. A changed policy version requires renewed agreement before further use. For non-sensitive questions or privacy complaints, contact the project owner through https://github.com/rcmiller01/hackertube/issues (available once the repository is public); never post credentials, private questions or personal history. Until publication, contact the person who supplied your private installation.
